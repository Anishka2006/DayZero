Project Brief

Astra SPA Migration

You are joining Astra's frontend platform team during an active migration sprint.

Company & Role

Company Astra
Your Role Frontend Engineer
Team Size 5 members
Timebox 3 hours

Project Background

Astra is moving a legacy account settings widget into a modern single page application shell. The migration is risky because the legacy widget still owns part of the routing logic, while the new SPA shell also manages navigation.

Your Goal

If handled poorly, users may experience duplicate route updates, page flicker, broken browser back/forward behavior, session refresh issues, or rollback failures.

Current Problems

  • Duplicate route updates
  • Remount flicker
  • Rollback instability
  • Session refresh bugs

Technical Context

The legacy widget owns legacyRouter subscriptions and directly calls window.history.pushState. The SPA shell owns BrowserRouter and route loading states. Session refresh is tied to sessionStore.touch() inside legacy navigation.

Files You Will Inspect

  • /src/widgets/LegacyWidget.js
  • /src/shell/SPAShell.jsx
  • /docs/migration-plan.md
  • /logs/router-errors.log

You Will

  • Choose a safe migration strategy
  • Inspect workspace files and identify blockers
  • Collaborate with teammates
  • Submit final handoff with decision, tradeoff, validation plan, and owner assignment

How You Will Be Evaluated

  • Leadership: Taking ownership of the migration decision
  • Communication: Clearly explaining tradeoffs to stakeholders
  • Technical Depth: Understanding routing and session risks
  • Prioritization: Focusing on the most critical blockers
  • Adaptability: Adjusting strategy based on team feedback
